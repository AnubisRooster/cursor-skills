# Voice examples

Facts are illustrative. Copy the rhythm, not the tickets.

## Email (less formal)

**Don't**

> I hope this message finds you well. I wanted to take a moment to reach out regarding our ongoing challenges with developer productivity. It is important to note that credit constraints are impacting velocity across the organization. Additionally, I would love to align on a comprehensive path forward. Please don't hesitate to reach out with any questions.

**Do**

> Chris, Qigang, Good day.
>
> The problem: LATC devs do not have enough Cursor credits to keep development velocity. The budget picture will not improve any time soon. Possibly next year's budget, not before.
>
> In the interim we need models that are accessible, performant, regionally permissible, and cost efficient. We would prefer to self serve from our own fleet, Model Factory, so we conserve AWS and Cursor spend.
>
> Can we align on what we can pursue with ATP, Model Pillar, Runtime, and Infrastructure? Coding harness, coding models, and inference. It is a cross pillar effort.
>
> If we wait, more people sit idle against a frozen credit pool.
>
> Thanks,
> Mike

## Confluence (formal)

**Don't**

> # A Holistic View of Inference Enablement
>
> In today's rapidly evolving AI landscape, it is critical that we leverage our internal assets to deliver a seamless developer experience. This page outlines key takeaways and a robust path forward.

**Do**

> ## Problem
>
> LATC developers cannot sustain coding-agent velocity on the current Cursor credit allocation. The budget will not recover this fiscal year.
>
> ## Ask
>
> Decide how LATC can serve coding models and inference from the Model Factory fleet, with ATP, Model Pillar, Runtime, and Infrastructure each naming the work they own.
>
> ## Risk if we don't
>
> Development velocity stays capped. AWS and Cursor spend continue to absorb work that the on-prem fleet could take. Regional access stays unclear for teams that cannot use the current path.
>
> ## Scope
>
> Coding harness, coding models, and inference. Self-serve first. Cloud credits as overflow, not the default.
